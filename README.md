# About Me:
Hi, I'm Arpan Biswas — a Computer Science undergraduate specializing in AI and ML at Haldia Institute of Technology. I spend most of my time building and shipping GenAI projects, usually with a hackathon deadline attached.<br><br>My work spans a fairly wide range: I've built a GAN-based cloud removal and reconstruction system for LISS-IV satellite imagery as part of ISRO's Bharatiya Antariksh Hackathon (BAH 2026), an adaptive AI technical interview agent that conducts personalized, multi-turn interviews grounded in a candidate's actual learning history, and ArenaX — an AI-powered chess platform with a real-time AI tutor and multiple engine difficulties. Alongside these, I've picked up hands-on experience deploying real applications with FastAPI, Docker, and Google Cloud Run, and I've competed across several other hackathons along the way, including UIDAI Hackathon 2026 and Hackarena.<br><br>Right now, I'm exploring agentic AI systems more deeply — working with Google's Agent Development Kit (ADK), Gemini, and the Model Context Protocol (MCP) through GeeksforGeeks × Google for Developers workshops. I'm especially drawn to projects where AI has to actually reason and make decisions in real time, not just answer questions — that's the thread connecting most of what I build, whether it's an interview agent evaluating a candidate's understanding or a chess engine deciding its next move.<br><br>I learn best by building under pressure, so most of what's in my repos started life as a hackathon project I kept pushing on afterward.
